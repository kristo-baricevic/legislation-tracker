## Facts and required answers

### COLA-F01 — title, purpose, affected groups; section 1 and enactment-purpose caption

The bill increases disability compensation and dependency/indemnity compensation for the specified veterans and survivors; its two short titles are given below. It is not a general increase for every veterans program.

> This Act may be cited as the ``Veterans' Compensation Cost-of- Living Adjustment Act of 2023'' or the ``Veterans' COLA Act of 2023''.

> To increase, effective as of December 1, 2023, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled veterans, and for other purposes.

### COLA-F02 — mandatory publication and externally keyed deadline; section 3

VA must publish the section 2(b) adjusted amounts in the Federal Register by the externally specified Social Security publication deadline associated with a determination during FY2024. The text does not supply a calendar date for that publication deadline.

> The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall publish in the Federal Register the amounts specified in section 2(b), as increased under that section, not later than the date on which the matters specified in section 215(i)(2)(D) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 415(i)(2)(D)) are required to be published by reason of a determination made under section 215(i) of such Act during fiscal year 2024.

## Every explicit financial provision

### COLA-M01 — shared mandatory amount formula, baseline, and timing; section 2(a), (c)

For COLA-M02–M06, increase the dollar amounts effective November 30, 2023, starting December 1, 2023, by the same percentage as the specified Social Security title II increase effective December 1, 2023, resulting from section 215(i). Neither a numerical percentage nor resulting dollars nor an aggregate appropriation is provided. No sunset is stated. The exact baseline amounts and percentage are external dependencies, not zero or guessed values.

> Effective on December 1, 2023, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall increase, in accordance with subsection (c), the dollar amounts in effect on November 30, 2023, for the payment of disability compensation and dependency and indemnity compensation under the provisions specified in subsection (b).

> Each dollar amount described in subsection (b) shall be increased by the same percentage as the percentage by which benefit amounts payable under title II of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 401 et seq.) are increased effective December 1, 2023, as a result of a determination under section 215(i) of such Act (42 U.S.C. 415(i)).

### COLA-M02 — wartime disability compensation; section 2(b)(1)

Purpose: raise each section 1114 amount, limited to that referenced provision; amount and timeframe: COLA-M01. Eligibility details and starting dollar amounts are not reproduced.

> Wartime disability compensation.--Each of the dollar amounts under section 1114 of title 38, United States Code.

### COLA-M03 — additional dependent compensation; section 2(b)(2)

Purpose: raise each section 1115(1) amount; restriction: that paragraph, not all of section 1115. Amount/timeframe: COLA-M01. Underlying eligibility unresolved.

> Additional compensation for dependents.--Each of the dollar amounts under section 1115(1) of such title.

### COLA-M04 — clothing allowance; section 2(b)(3)

Purpose: raise the section 1162 clothing allowance; amount/timeframe: COLA-M01. The underlying allowance rules and dollars are unresolved.

> Clothing allowance.--The dollar amount under section 1162 of such title.

### COLA-M05 — surviving-spouse dependency/indemnity compensation; section 2(b)(4)

Purpose: raise each section 1311(a)–(d) amount; amount/timeframe: COLA-M01; restriction: specified subsections, not every possible survivor benefit.

> Dependency and indemnity compensation to surviving spouse.--Each of the dollar amounts under subsections (a) through (d) of section 1311 of such title.

### COLA-M06 — children's dependency/indemnity compensation; section 2(b)(5)

Purpose: raise each amount in sections 1313(a) and 1314; amount/timeframe: COLA-M01; restriction: those provisions. Underlying eligibility unresolved.

> Dependency and indemnity compensation to children.--Each of the dollar amounts under sections 1313(a) and 1314 of such title.

### COLA-M07 — discretionary special-rule compensation adjustment; section 2(d)

VA may administratively adjust disability-compensation rates for the specified Public Law 85-857 section 10 recipients who have not received title 38 chapter 11 compensation. Adjustment must be consistent with section 2(a)'s increases. No separate dollars, percentage, or independent date is supplied; this is “may,” not the mandatory “shall” applicable to COLA-M01–M06.

> The Secretary of Veterans Affairs may adjust administratively, consistent with the increases made under subsection (a), the rates of disability compensation payable to persons under section 10 of Public Law 85-857 (72 Stat. 1263) who have not received compensation under chapter 11 of title 38, United States Code.
