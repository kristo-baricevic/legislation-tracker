## Purpose and people affected — checked slice only

### P01 — This is a multi-policy reconciliation law, not just a military funding bill

The long title states the reconciliation purpose. The checked sections below
change nutrition-benefit rules, provide military-personnel funding, rescind
certain earlier housing-program balances, and fund other named activities.
Those examples do not exhaust the law. Section 1's contents span Titles I–X;
an accepted whole-bill synopsis still requires review of the remaining titles.

Source: long title.

> To provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of H. Con. Res. 14.

### P02 — SNAP benefit calculations and recipients are affected (§10101)

The law rewrites the thrifty-food-plan definition. Household allotments use
specified size ratios; annual inflation adjustment remains distinct from
re-evaluating the food basket. Do not say that benefits are frozen at a nominal
dollar value: the basket-re-evaluation restriction is not the annual CPI rule.

Source: §10101(a), replacement §3(u)(1)(B), (3)(C), (4)(B).

> the cost of the thrifty food plan shall be the basis for uniform allotments for all households, regardless of the actual composition of the household;

> on October 1, 2025, and on each October 1 thereafter, adjust the cost of the thrifty food plan to reflect changes in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the Department of Labor, for the most recent 12-month period ending in June.

> The Secretary shall not increase the cost of the thrifty food plan based on a re-evaluation under this paragraph.

### P03 — Work-rule exemptions cannot be reduced to “all adults must work” (§10102)

The replacement exemption list includes people under 18 or over 65, people
medically certified unfit for employment, a household member responsible for a
dependent child under 14, pregnant women, and specified Indigenous groups.
There is also a cross-reference exemption whose full meaning is unresolved here.
These are exemptions from the referenced paragraph (2), not a complete standalone
restatement of SNAP eligibility. Preserve the source's strict “over 65” wording.

Source: §10102(a), replacement §6(o)(3)(A)–(E).

> ``(3) Exceptions.--Paragraph (2) shall not apply to an individual if the individual is-- ``(A) under 18, or over 65, years of age; ``(B) medically certified as physically or mentally unfit for employment; ``(C) a parent or other member of a household with responsibility for a dependent child under 14 years of age; ``(D) otherwise exempt under subsection (d)(2); ``(E) a pregnant woman;

Source: §10102(a), replacement §6(o)(3)(F).

> an Indian or an Urban Indian (as such terms are defined in paragraphs (13) and (28) of section 4 of the Indian Health Care Improvement Act)

Source: §10102(a), replacement §6(o)(3)(G).

> a California Indian described in section 809(a) of the Indian Health Care Improvement Act.

### P04 — Internet fees are excluded from a particular SNAP expense deduction (§10104)

Do not describe this as a prohibition on purchasing internet access or as a new
internet fee. The law excludes the expense from a benefit-calculation deduction.

> Any service fee associated with internet connection shall not be used in computing the excess shelter expense deduction under this paragraph.

### M17 — §20001(b): a temporary investment ratio, not another appropriation

From enactment through September 30, 2029, substitute 60% for two percentages in
the referenced privatized-military-housing investment provision: 33⅓% and 45%.
The dollar base and full legal operation require the pinned prior version of
10 USC 2875(c), not just this amendment. Do not turn either percentage into a
new dollar allocation or assume 60% of M01–M16.

> beginning on the date of the enactment of this section and ending on September 30, 2029, the Secretary concerned shall apply-- (A) paragraph (1) of subsection (c) of section 2875 of title 10, United States Code, by substituting ``60 percent'' for ``33 \1/3\ percent''; and (B) paragraph (2) of such subsection by substituting ``60 percent'' for ``45 percent''.

### M20–M21 — §60025: Kennedy Center capital work and administrative cap

M20: Actual additional FY2025 appropriation of **$256,657,000**, available through
September 30, 2029, for the specified building/site repair, restoration,
maintenance backlog, and security structures. M21: **not more than 3% of that
appropriation** may cover necessary administration. M21 is a subset cap, not
additional funding or a guaranteed expenditure.

> $256,657,000, to remain available until September 30, 2029, for necessary expenses for capital repair, restoration, maintenance backlog, and security structures of the building and site of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts.

> Of the amounts made available under subsection (a), not more than 3 percent may be used for administrative costs necessary to carry out this section.

### M22 — §60026 / added NEPA §112: optional environmental-review fee

A project sponsor may opt to pay **125% of anticipated review-preparation costs**;
for sponsor-prepared work the applicable base is anticipated supervision and,
where applicable, preparation costs. This is a formula, not a fixed dollar fee.
The Council supplies notice of the fee within 15 days after receiving the
specified project information. A paid environmental assessment must finish within
180 days after payment; a paid environmental impact statement within one year
after publication of the notice of intent. Do not give both the payment-date
deadline or imply paying the fee guarantees project approval.

Source: added §112(a)(3), (a)(4), (b).

> A project sponsor may pay a fee under this section after receipt of the notice described in paragraph (2).

> an environmental assessment for which a fee is paid under this section shall be completed not later than 180 days after the date on which the fee is paid;

> an environmental impact statement for which a fee is paid under this section shall be completed not later than 1 year after the date of publication of the notice of intent to prepare the environmental impact statement.

> 125 percent of the anticipated costs to prepare the environmental assessment or environmental impact statement;

> in the case of an environmental assessment or environmental impact statement to be prepared in whole or in part by a project sponsor under section 107(f), 125 percent of the anticipated costs to supervise preparation of, and, as applicable, prepare, the environmental assessment or environmental impact statement.
